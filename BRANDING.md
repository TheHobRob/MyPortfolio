## Voice / Personality
Derived from birth chart read (Capricorn Sun, Aquarius stellium, Sagittarius MC) — use as a gut-check when writing, not a rulebook:
- Dry wit over enthusiasm — avoid "hey guys, welcome back!" energy
- Understated confidence — let content speak, don't oversell
- Values originality — okay to be a little unconventional, avoid generic blog-voice
- Warmth shows through specificity/detail, not gushing
- Sagittarius streak: comfortable teaching / sharing what you've learned, not just reviewing
