My First Post

By Roberto Jimenez-Martinez

Hello World! This is my first official post. Join me as I share my journey, experiences, and insights as I return to the world of coding, web development and beyond.

My Journey

I started my coding journey back in 2015 in high school during an Introduction to Computer Science course 💻. Developing little flash games in the beautifully designed programming language: Scratch 🐱. I was hooked instantly. My logical brain was tickled and finally, I thought, I understood what a Computer is capable of. Then came 2017, I graduated high school and pursued a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science at the College of Engineering in the University of Michigan 🟨🟦. This was great! Until it wasn’t. Life started to become very real, very quickly. Then all of the sudden: 💥boom💥! It’s 2020.

I was contemplating my decision of having pursued a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science. Many questions came to my mind: Do I still love this?❣️ Am I capable of staying up to date with technology?💻 Is my career flashing before my eyes?👁️ Why am I so far from home?🏠 Who am I?🧍 The world outside was crumbling and so was mine. I gave it my best effort given the circumstances and ultimately withdrew from university in 2022. I have since moved back home to West Michigan, working jobs to pay the bills. 🧾 You know how it is. Then came 2026. And after having worked in the real world for 4 years, all the roads I took lead me back to coding. 💻 I started working with my computer, I designed a program, and then I organized some data in beautiful layouts. I began to do it for fun again, as a hobby. I came back to the feeling that coding gave me 11 years ago in my introduction class in high school. Now here we are, with all my experience on hand. 🫰 I began to code and I asked myself:

Why Not Make a Website?

Making a website about myself requires so many skills beyond those I was developing at University.🤓 I knew what my spirit wanted and it was not figuring out the science of computation. I want to use computation to show its integration into everyday life.🔢 Many days I spend thinking, logically. Mathematically as Finn would say. So I ended up here, creating my portfolio on a website. This is great for a now drop-out computer scientist.🙂 Coding websites doesn’t have to make sense to a computer, it has to make sense to me, a human.🧍 A website has to act like a little window into the world of a brand 🌎, a company 🏢, a business 🌐, a product, etcetera. It’s a piece on the internet that is entirely my own.🌌
